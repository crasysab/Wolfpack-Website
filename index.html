WHAT WE REVIEW

Your marketing should be tested before the market tests it for you.

Developers know their own game too well to experience the store page like a new player. Wolfpack creates a controlled first impression and turns it into feedback you can act on.

01

Store page

Does the page communicate the genre, hook and value quickly enough to earn another click?

  • Click intent
  • Genre clarity
  • Wishlist intent
02

Trailer

Where does attention rise or fall, and how quickly does the trailer show what playing feels like?

  • Opening impact
  • Gameplay clarity
  • Drop-off points
03

Visual assets

Test capsule art and screenshots for readability, positioning and the expectations they create.

  • Capsule appeal
  • Visual hierarchy
  • Player expectation
04

Demo first look

Put the opening experience in front of relevant players and identify friction while it can still be fixed.

  • Onboarding
  • Confusion points
  • Play-again intent

HOW IT WORKS

A simple process. A clearer outside view.

01

Submit the content

Send the page, trailer, images or demo you want reviewed and tell us what you need to learn.

02

We match the reviewers

We select gamers whose genre preferences and player profile are relevant to the project.

03

They review independently

Each reviewer follows the same structured brief, giving us responses we can compare properly.

04

You receive the report

We group the patterns, points of friction and written comments into one practical summary.
