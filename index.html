QUESTIONS

Clear expectations before a project starts.

Is Wolfpack a public review site?

No. Wolfpack is a private feedback service for developers. We do not publish public scores or reviews as part of these packages.

How are reviewers selected?

Reviewers are matched using the genres, platforms and playing habits they give us when joining the pool. Matching is handled manually during the founding stage.

Are reviewers paid?

Yes. Paid assignments show the expected task and reward before a reviewer accepts. Compensation is for thoughtful completion, not for giving positive feedback.

Can I submit unreleased material?

Yes, where the project and access method are appropriate. Private material is handled separately from public-page reviews, and additional confidentiality terms can be agreed when needed.

How quickly will I receive the report?

We confirm a delivery date before accepting the project. Reviewer availability and the scope of the material affect timing, so we do not promise a generic automated turnaround.

Does a review guarantee more wishlists or sales?

No. Player feedback is directional research. It helps you understand how a relevant sample experiences the material; product and marketing decisions remain yours.
